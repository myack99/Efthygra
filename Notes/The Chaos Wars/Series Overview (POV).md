# POV Options
Originally I had planned to have each book be from the POV of a different protagonist (as denoted above in o1). However, I have been questioning he best way to present the story.

---

    Option 1
* Each book has 1 POV
* Each book is a new POV
* The characters cross over, so it is not a brand new character when it is theri turn to narrate (exception: Kaelahaas)
* Epilogue of each book is from POV of next book's POV

|Pros|Cons|
|-|-|
|Each book gets a unique style|Forced into only one POV per book|
|Get to fully explore each character across whole book|Some books could have a "worse" POV than others|
|Only have to focus on inner thoughts on one character||

---

    Option 2
* Each book has multiple POVs (as denoted above in o2)
* i.e. Book 1 is split POV of Rowan/Taron/Breah/Liaeyra
  * Liaeyra potenitally has some chapters from her POV in every book

|Pros|Cons|
|-|-|
|Not stuck with only one POV|Harder to manage when to use which POV|
|Ability to explore different angles||

---

    Option 3
* Liaeyra is POV of all 6 books

|Pros|Cons|
|-|-|
|Get to grow with Liaeyra for the whole series|Forced into one persepctive for whole series|
|Don't have to worry about some books having "less interesting" POVs||
|Only have to focus on inner thoughts on one character||

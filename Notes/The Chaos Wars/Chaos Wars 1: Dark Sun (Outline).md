# Outline (Previous Google Docs Entry)
**Outline:**

Act 1
* Followers of Taynso vs followers of Tanysa. Act ends with Tanyso taking his powers away from Rowan after they question him about not being able to heal Tanysa’s followers.
* 

Act 2
* Rowan joins the Steelan cult and begins working with the Crowe siblings. Act ends with Steelan speaking to Rowan.
* 

Act 3
* Rowan and Steelan put their plan into action, slowly corrupting each other. Act ends with Rowan killing Finn, completing their irreversible path into darkness.
* 

Act 4
* The final act shows the execution of Rowan’s plan and the start of the Chaos Wars.

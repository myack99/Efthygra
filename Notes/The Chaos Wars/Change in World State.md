# Summary
I started developing Efthygra for the context of a DND campaign set several thousand years after the Chaos Wars. Therefore, the world state would be very different during the time of the Chaos Wars Book Series. This document contains my notes on the change in world state.

## Original Thoughts
    Copied from an old Google Doc on 5/19/26:

This series takes place before/during the Re-Emergence, so there are things in my established lore that are very different.

Obviously, dates are denoted as BR, AR until after the Re-Emergence established this.
* Dates are based upon eras
* The current era at the start of the Chaos Wars is the “Age of ______”

The calendar is also pre-shift, meaning that Kordaecath is the 1st month

At this time, the Kingdoms are also different
* Dokartol is an unimportant island with no real government
  * During the Dark Ages, the Cinderuga Clan travels there and established a Royal Family
* The Tegem Isles are all separate Kingdoms, united through trade
  * During the Chaos Wars, there was a power struggle, as Kingdoms fought for control of the prime Kingdom
  * During the Dark Ages, the ruling power subjugated the other islands and established a monarchy. There is now constant turmoil within the isles and they ignore outsiders.
* Chorvas and Toallo are broken it various city states, with a democratic alliance uniting them

The Chaos Wars are a hard reset on society
* Pre-society is far more advanced; think futuristic high fantasy
* The War sets the world back centuries

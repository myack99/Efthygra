## Channel / Strait
* narrow body of water that connects 2 larger bodies of water
* typically deeper than surrounding water
* Connecting Channels: 
  * wide waterways that separate 2 landmasses and link larger bodies of water
* Navigation Channels:
  * deep, often dredged corridors in shallow bays. etec.
* River Channels:
  * natural physical path that a river flows through
* Strait:
  * often narrower
* **CLIMATE**: any

Maybe occurs in:
* coastal areas
* Tegem
* bewtween Mokupi and Nerovathia

## Marsh
* low-lying, poorly drained wetland
* common at edges of lakes and streams
  * act as transition between aquatic and terrestrial ecosystems
* Marsh vs beach:
  * Beaches from on sloped shores, where sediment can be deposited
  * Marshes are in shallow flat areas that stay submerged and dont allow for sediment to move
* essentially watery grasslands
* **CLIMATE**: any

Maybe occurs in:
* near water

## Sea
* large body of saltwater that is smaller/shallower than an ocean
* partially enclosed by land

Sea vs Bay vs Gulf vs Bight
* thes are basically interchangeable
* Sea is typically larger than a Gulf which is typically larger than a Bay
* Sea is typically less enclosed than a gulf which is less enclosed than a bay
* Bights are essentially broad bays

**CLIMATE**: any

Maybe occurs in:
* many places?

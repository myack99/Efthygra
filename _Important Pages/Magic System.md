# Schools of Magic
## [[Amethyne Magic]]
The most common way of casting magic. It requires the mage to become attuned to an Amethyst. They use this Amethyst to channel their connection to the Amethyne Fabric of Magic, so that they can manipulate it to cast spells. Attempting to cast a spell with an Amethyst that one is not attuned to, may have varying levels of success and can cause abnormalities to occur.

**[[Abjuration]]** - Spells used for protection

**[[Conjuration]]** - Spells used for creating objects

**[[Divination]]** - Spells used for revealing knowledge

**[[Enchantment]]** - Spells used to affect the minds of others

**[[Evocation]]** - Spells used to create energy

**[[Illusion]]** - Spells used to deceive others

**[[Necromancy]]** - Spells used to manipulate death energy

**[[Transmutation]]** - Spells used to transform objects/creatures/environments

**[[Summoning]]** - Spells used to summon creatures

## [[Elemental Magic]]
Elemental Magic is derived from the elements that are the foundation for Efthygra (Earth, Air, Water, Fire). This is done either by becoming one with the chosen element [^1] or by taking years to study and attune with the natural element itself (Earth magic - sometimes reffered to as Nature Magic - is the most common of these, although the others do occur).

## [[Pact Magic]]
Uses pacted Material Components (MC) to cast spells. These comonents are offerings to a Patron of some sort, for access to their magic. This Patron is typically a powerful Deity or Other Being, that possesses Divine or Archaic Magic, although it can also sometimes be a powerful mortal mage. This makes Pact Magic, less of it's own unique School of Magic, and more of a different way to access other Schools of Magic.

**[[Blood Magic | Pact of Blood]]** - Blood (either the Mage's or someoe else's) is used as the MC

**[[Riches Magic | Pact of Riches]]** - Valuable gems and currencies are used as the MC

**[[Sacrifice Magic | Pact of Sacrifice]]** - The sacrifice of other beings is used as the MC

**[[Knowledge Magic | Pact of Knowledge]]** - Information is used as the MC

## [[Divine Magic]]
This is the Magic that the Gods inherently possess. It is separate from the Amethyne[^2], and is not accessible by mortals unless granted  to them by one of these Gods.

## [[Archaic Magic]]
This is the Magic that is inherently possessed by powerful beings from other plnaes of existence (i.e. The Hells). Similarly to Divine magic, this is not accessible by mortlas, unless granted to them by one of these beings. While it is theoretically possible for a mage to enter into an agreement with one of these beings in a simiar way as a Cleric or Paladin, it is virtually unheard of. Mortals typically can only access this magic through Pact Magic.

It is, though rare, also possible for some mortals to be born innately with this Magic. Under very rare and specific circumstances, this can occur, and create what would be commonly known as a Wild Magic Sorceror.

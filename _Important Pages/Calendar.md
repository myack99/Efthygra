# Efthygran Calendar
One year consists of 416 days.
Each year has 13 months, composed of 4 weeks each. Each week has 8 days.

One day lasts 24 hours[^1].

The date is referenced in the format:

    [ordinal number] [day of the week] of [month], [year]
    Example: The Second Dathnoresh of Lesherecath, 1207 AR
    Informally -> 2 Dath, Lesherecath, 1207
## The Great Fire as Year 0 (DE / ME)
Dates are based around [[The Great Fire]] as year 0. This is when the Gods retreated from Efthygra and became less involved in mortal affairs.
Dates before The Great Fire are denoted as the **Divine Era (DE)** and go up in number the further back in time the years go.
Dates after The Great Fire are denoted as the **Mortal Era (ME)** and go up in number the further forward in time the years go.
Unless DE is specified, it is assumed that the year is ME. As is such, the ME is usually excluded in the naming of the date.

## Re-Emergence as Year 0 (AR / BR)
Dates are based around the [[Re-Emergence]] as year 0. 
Dates before the Re-Emergence are denoted as **Before Re-Emergence (BR)** and go up in number the further back in time the years go.
Dates after the Re-Emergence are denoted as **After Re-Emergence (AR)** and go up in number the further forward in time the years go.
Unless BR is specified, it is assumed that the year is AR. As is such, the AR is usually excluded in the naming of the date.

## Conversion
Before the [[Re-Emergence]] occurred, time was denoted using the DE/ME system. After the Re-Emergence, the system switched to AR/BR. 

To convert from year BR to year DE/ME:

    4,766 - [year number (BR)] = [year number (DE/ME)]
    If resulting year is positive, the year is in ME
    If resulting year is negative, the year is in DE
    
To convert from year ME to BR:

    [year number (ME)] - 4,766 = [year number (AR)]
    
To convert from year DE to BR:

    [year number (DE)] + 4,766 = [year number (AR)]

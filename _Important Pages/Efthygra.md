# Description
**EFTHYGRA** is the World in which all of these stories take place. 

Efthygra was created in the year 14798 BR (5032 DE) during an event known as [[The Alignment]]. During this event, the first Gods were born of the four elements ([[Therdrin]] (Air), [[Orisia]] (Earth), [[Sopack]] (Water), [[Braruun]] (Fire)). Together they shaped the World of Efthygra and divided it into four domains. The [[Skies]], The [[Land]], The [[Oceans]], and The [[Core]]. Each God took residence in their new domain and set out to rule Efthygra.

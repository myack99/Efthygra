# Efthygran Pantheon
**The Efthygran Pantheon** consists of 13 [[Greater Deities]] and several [[Lesser Deities]]. 

## Greater Deities
The [[Greater Deities]] of Efthygra are the Divine Gods that helped to shape the world and everything in it. Each Greater Deity (simply referred to as "The Gods") has a Domain in the mortal realm that they reside in, as well as a home in the Heavens. Each domain is directly connectd to the innate magic that each God possesses. This magic is known as [[Divine Magic]] and is unique to each God. Gods can choose to channel their Divine Magic to their followers, though this is often reserved for devoted Priests and Clerics.

---

The Divine Pantheon are categorized into 5 categories.

### The First Gods

    [[Therdrin]] (Air) | [[Orisia]] (Earth) | [[Sopack]] (Water) | [[Braruun]] (Fire)

The [[First Gods]] were the ones born during [[The Alignment]]. They were born from the Elemental Planes of Earth, Air, Water, and Fire, and each God harnesses the power of one of these elements. They shaped the world of Efthygra, designing the four primary realms of the mortal world: The [[Skies]], The [[Lands]], The [[Oceans]], and The [[Core]].

After shaping Efthygra, the First Gods then created the first God of their own.

### The First Child

    [[Narre]] (Life)

The [[First Child]] was the first God created by the First Gods. Using the combined powers of air, earth, water, and fire, the four Gods made the Goddess Narre in their image. She was tasked with filling the newly created world with life. 

Eventually, she created the first mortal race, the [[Elf | Elves]]. She then created new Gods to help guide them.

### The Children of Narre

    [[Anchesena]] (Knowledge) | [[Morrom]] (Death) | [[Tanyso]] (The Sun) | [[Tanysa]] (The Moon)

The [[Children of Narre]] were the Gods created by Narre to hepl guide the Elves. Anchesena was created to grant them knoweldge and wisdom. With this, Braruun brought the knowledge of their own mortality and the ability for them to die. Tanyso represented the Sun and provided light, while Tanysa represented the Moon and provided darkness. All four Gods were devoted to the Elves in their ways.

### Gods of the First Sin

    [[Navay]] (Trickery) | [[Faeras]] (Magic) | [[Alusafa]] (War) | [[Steelan]] | (Fortune)

The [[Gods of the First Sin]] were the Gods born of the Elves lust for power. As a new generation of Elves came into being, they sought out more magic than what the Elements could provide them. They desired the magic of the Gods. Morrom spun a prophecy that promised to grant magic to a mortal. However, his words were deceiving and he cast his magic in the body of a dead Elf, creating the new Goddess of deceipt and trickery. The Goddess of deceipt then deceived her maker's purpose and cast off her own arm to create the God of Magic. Faeras brought magic to the Elves, which angered Therdrin and Braruun, causing them to create a new God of War, to seek vengeance on the mortal Elves. narre then created the God of Fate and Fortune to protect the Elves and bring them good fortune.

### The Ascended God

    [[Kaelahaas]] (The Sun)

[[The Ascended God]] is the God that ascended to Godhood after the  death of the original God of the Sun. Beginning as a mortal Elf[^1], Kaelahaas ascended to the Heavens, assuming the Divine Magic that had previously belonged to Tanyso.

## Lesser Deities

The [[Lesser Deities]] of Efthygra, also known as [[Aasimar]] or demigods, are the children of Greater Deities. They possess the power of their parent, but also take on their own power, creating a new form of [[Divine Magic]].

Lesser Deities, while t echnically mortal, are Gods in their own way. They age at a slower rate and are all but immortal to the consequences of time. However, unlike Greater Deities, they can be killed by mortal means.

# Realms of Efthygra
Efthygra is divided into 13 Realms.

There are the 4 **Elemental Realms**, the 2 **Outer Realms**, the 2 **Magical Realms**, the 2 **Solar Realms**, the 2 **Spiritual Realms**, and the infinite **Pocket Realms**.

There is also a 14th Realm, that exists beyond Efthygra.

## Elemental Realms
The Elemental Realms are the 4 Realms that make up the phyiscal world of Efthygra.
### The Skies
[[Skies |The Skies]] are the Realm making up all the space in the air of Efthygra. [[Therdrin]] is the God that rules in dominion over The Skies.

### The Lands
[[Land | The Lands]] are the Realm making up all the land of Efthygra. [[Orisia]] is the God that rules in dominion over The Lands.

### The Oceans
[[Oceans | The Oceans]] are the Realm making up all the oceans of Efthygra. [[Sopack]] is the God that rules in dominion over The Oceans.

### The Core
[[Core | The Core]] is the Realm making up the hot core at the center of the world of Efthygra. [[Braruun]] is the God that rules in dominion over The Core.

## Outer Realms
The Outer Realms are the 2 Realms dedicated to the Gods. No living mortal is able to enter into these Realms.

### The Heavens
[[The Heavens]] are the Realm that serves a a home for the Gods of Efthygra. While each God presides over their own Realm, this Realm is where they live and there is no one God that holds dominion over the whole Realm. No mortal is able to enter into The Heavens.

### The Hells
[[The Hells]] are the Realm that serves as the afterlife for mortal life on Efthygra. It is also home to various Hellspawn. [[Morrom]] is the God that rules in dominion over The Hells.

## Magical Realms
The Magical Realms are the 2 Realms that exist allongside Efthygra, in their own parallel pockets of space-time. They mirror the Primary Realms, yet exist on their own. The 2 Magical Realms heavily bleed into each other.

### The Magic Realm
[[Amethyne Realm | The Amethyne Realm]] is the Magical Realm that sprang into existence when [[Faeras]] shaped the elemental fabric of Efthygra into the [[Amethyne Fabric of Magic]]. This Realm is the source of the Amethyne's power. Faeras is the God that rules in dominion over the Magic Realm.

### The Umbra Realm
[[Umbra Realm | The Umbra Realm]] is the Magical Realm that sprang into existence in opposition to the Magic Realm. While the Magic Realm is the source of the Amethyne, the Umbra Realm is a source of dark magic and is the entity that allowed the use of [[Pact Magic]] to become possible. [[Navay]] is the God that rules in dominion over the Magic Realm.

## Solar Realms
The Solar Realms are the 2 Realms that are occupied by the Sun and the Moon. They exist in the same space-time as Efthygra, though lie outside its boundaires.

### The Sun
[[The Sun]] is the Realm that amkes up Efthygra's Sun. [[Tanyso]] (and later [[Kaelahaas]]) is the God that rules in dominion over The Sun.

### The Moon
[[The Moon]] is the Realm that amkes up Efthygra's Moon. [[Tanysa]] is the God that rules in dominion over The Moon.

## Spiritual Realms
The Spiritual Realms are the 2 Realms that exist fully outside of the space-time of Efthygra.

### The Astral Realm
[[Astral Realm | The Astral Realm]] is the Realm that exists outside of space-time. It is accessed through astral projection of the spirit. [[Anchesena]] is the God that rules in dominion over The Astral Realm

### The Ethereal Realm
[[Ethereal Realm | The Ethereal Realm]] is the Realm that manipulates space-time. It is a parallel-esque version of Efthygra that does not follow the laws of space or time. When someone taps their spirit into the Ethereal Relam, they temporarily pass out of existence, before re-emerging, instantly. [[Steelan]] is the God that rules in dominion over The Ethereal Realm.

## Pocket Realms
[[Pocket Realms | The Pocket Realms]] are the infinite Realms that exist in small, parallel pockets of space-time.

### The Prime Pocket Realm
[[Prime Pocket Realm | The Prime Pocket Realm]] is the Pocket Realm that all other Pocket Realms gain power from. Without it, all other Pockets would collapse. [[Narre]] created this Realms to develop new creations away from the wrath of the other Gods, and she rules in dominoin over The Pocket Realms.

## The Void
[[The Void]] is the Realm encompassing the Void of Space surrounding Efthygra. No God holds dominion here and it is only occupied by ancient creatures that existed before [[The Alignment]].

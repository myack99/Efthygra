# Oceans
## Pagosim Ocean
* Freeze -> pagoma (Greek) + North -> sim (Elvish)

roughly above 50* North

## Toallan Ocean
covers roughly East to West border of Toallo

## Arnic Ocean
* South -> arn(Elvish)

everything south of the furthest south island

## Thalusas Ocean
* sea -> thalassa (Greek) + ocean -> alu (Elvish)

the largest ocean, covers all other areas

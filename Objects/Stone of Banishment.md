# Description
**THE STONE OF BANISHMENT** was the Jade stone that was used to imprison [[Steelan]] during [[The Chaos Wars]].

Location: In the possession of [[Pasifae]] (stolen from the [[White Crescent]] vault during the campaign)
* An ancient jade gemstone, forged during the Chaos Wars to banish Steelan from Efthygra, this Stone has the power to banish anything. By itself, the Stone can cast the Banishment spell at 9th level. However, when coupled with the Crypt of the Underworld, the Stone of Banishment can cast Plane Shift to banish all creatures that stand within the Marked Sigil. The selected plane of banishment is determined by tuning the Ivory Altar to that plane (this acts as the material component). Once used, in any capacity, the Stone takes 24 hours to recharge.

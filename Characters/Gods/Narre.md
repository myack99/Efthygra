# Description
**NARRE** is the Efthygan Goddess of Life and Creation. Narre came into being in the year 9744 BR (5008 DE) when the First Gods ([[Therdrin]], [[Sopack]], [[Braruun]], and [[Orisia]]) worked together to create one of their own.

Narre, being gifted the domain of Life, began filling the world with her creations. After creating the animals, she eventually created the first sapient race, the [[Elf | Elves]]. The Elves were created in the image of the Gods and Narre cared for them greatly. She taught them the ways of land and even how to harness the [[Elemental Magic | Elemental Power]] of Efthygra. Eventually, she decided to create new Gods to assist her in caring for her creations and thus [[Anchesena]] (Knowledge), [[Morrom]] (Death), [[Tanyso]] (Sun and Light), and [[Tanysa]] (Moon and Darkness) were born.

When the God [[Faeras]] brought [[Amethyne Magic | Magic]] to the Elves, Therdrin and Braruun forged the God of War, [[Alusafa]] to retaliate. Narre fought hard, alongside  [[Faeras]], [[Steelan]], [[Navay]], [[Tanyso]], and [[Tanysa]], to protect her creations, however she feared it was a losing battle. She created a new race, the [[Gnome | Gnomes]], designed to be smaller and better and hiding, as an attmept to preserve mortal life. She also created the God of Fate and Fortune, [[Steelan]], to be a shield to the Elves. Eventually, [[The First Divine War]] ended and the Elves reamined present of Efthygra. 

Narre comtinued to create life on Efthygra for the next several millennia, until the fate of all mortal life was threatened by Braruun during [[The Great Fire]]. Sopack, Orisia, and Steelan worked alongside narre to push back the fires and save the continent of [[Chorvas]]. This exertion of energy, however, cause Narre to retreat to the Heavens alongside all the other Gods, thus ending the [[Divine Era]].

When [[The Chaos Wars]] began, Narre was one the first Gods to get involved, appalled that Steelan, whom she had created as a protector, would take the life of another God. She fought in the Wars, seeking to preserve as much life as possible, not reatreating until Steelan was imprisoned.

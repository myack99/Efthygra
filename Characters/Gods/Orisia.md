# Description
**ORISIA** is the Efthygran Goddess of the Earth. Orisia came into being during the creation of Efthygra, known as the [[The Alignment]] (11798 BR (7032 DE)). Born of the Elemental plane of fire alongside [[Therdrin]] (Air), [[Sopack]] (Water), and [[Braruun]] (Fire), the First Gods shaped the World of Efthygra. Orisia took of residence in the Lands, which became her domain on Efthygra, while Therdrin took the skies, Sopack the oceans, and Braruun the [[Core]].

In 9774 BR (5008 DE), the First Gods worked together to create one of their own. [[Narre]] was the first child of the Gods, imbued with the power of Life itself. 

In 4766 BR (0 DE/ME), Braruun sent his power through the UnderCore out into the Earth he caused mountains to erupt into flames, creating the first volcanoes. One of these volcanoes, [[Mount Ash]] spread so much fire, that it threatened to burn down the entire continent and everything in it. Only the combined efforts of Orisia, Narre, Sopack, and [[Steelan]] were able to push back the fires and save the continent. This event, know as [[The Great Fire]] lead to the last remaining Gods on Efthygra to fully retreat into the Heavens, thus ending the [[Divine Era]].

When [[The Chaos Wars]] began, Orisia fought to preserve the land and was instrumental in the resurgence of [[Elemental Magic]] during the War. She retreated back to the Heavens when Steelan was imprisoned.

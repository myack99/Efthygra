# Description
**THERDIN** is the Efthygran God of Air. Therdrin came into being during the creation of Efthygra, known as the [[The Alignment]] (11798 BR (7032 DE)). Born of the Elemental plane of air alongside [[Braruun]] (Fire), [[Sopack]] (Water), and [[Orisia]] (Earth), the First Gods shaped the World of Efthygra. Therdrin took residence in the skies, which became his domain on Efthygra, while Sopack took the oceans, Orisia the Earth, and Braruun the [[Core]].

In 9774 BR (5008 DE), the First Gods worked together to create one of their own. [[Narre]] was the first child of the Gods, imbued with the power of Life itself. 

In the year 6750 BR (1984 DE), the newly created God [[Faeras]] granted magic to the [[Elf | Elves]], which greatly angered Braruun and Therdrin. As a response, Braruun forged [[Alusafa]] out of stone and Therdrin breathed life into him. Alusafa was born with the sole purpose of enacting revenge on the Elves. This was the start of [[The First Divine War]]. 

During the War, Alusafa and Braruun lead legions of Dark Creatures against Narre and her Elves. Most of these creatures were sculpted by Braruun and given life by Therdin ([[Dragon | Dragons]], [[Orc | Orcs]], [[Goblin | Goblins]], etc.). Therdrin backed Alusafa and Braruun, though he did not engage in the War as much. Eventually Braruun grew bored of fighting and the War ended.

When [[The Chaos Wars]] began, Therdrin vowed to seek justice for the death of a fellow God. He was enraged the a mortal could gain such power and that a God would assist them. He vowed to make Steelan suffer and aimed to rid the world of mortals. When Therdrin learned of the plan to imprison Steelan, he decided to assist, deeming it a worthy punishment for the Betrayer God. With Steelan imprisoned in the [[Stone of Banishment]], he retreated to the Heavens, sealing the stone away.

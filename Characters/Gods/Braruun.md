# Description
**BRARUUN** is the Efthygran God of Fire. Braruun came into being during the creation of Efthygra, known as the [[The Alignment]] (11798 BR (7032 DE)). Born of the Elemental plane of fire alongside [[Therdrin]] (Air), [[Sopack]] (Water), and [[Orisia]] (Earth), the First Gods shaped the World of Efthygra. Braruun took of residence in the [[Core]], which became his domain on Efthygra, while Therdrin took the skies, Sopack the oceans, and Orisia the Earth.

In 9774 BR (5008 DE), the First Gods worked together to create one of their own. [[Narre]] was the first child of the Gods, imbued with the power of Life itself. 

In the year 6750 BR (1984 DE), the newly created God [[Faeras]] granted magic to the [[Elf | Elves]], which greatly angered Braruun and Therdrin. As a response, Braruun forged [[Alusafa]] out of stone and Therdrin breathed life into him. Alusafa was born with the sole purpose of enacting revenge on the Elves. This was the start of [[The First Divine War]]. 

During the War, Alusafa and Braruun lead legions of Dark Creatures against Narre and her Elves. Most of these creatures were sculpted by Braruun and given life by Therdin ([[Dragon | Dragons]], [[Orc | Orcs]], [[Goblin | Goblins]], etc.). Some of these were Elves stolen to the UnderCore, where they were corrupted into [[Dark Elf | Dark Elves]]. Therdrin backed Alusafa and Braruun, though he did not engage in the War as much. Eventually Braruun grew bored of fighting and retreated from the War bac kto the UnderCore.

As Narre continued to create life on Efthygra, Braruun grew frustrated with the worship she received. He continued to make his own creations, however no longer angered by the War, Therdrin no longer breathed life into them. Braruun grew angry at these circumstances until eventually he exploded. Sending his power through the UnderCore out into the Earth he caused mountains to erupt into flames, creating the first volcanoes. One of these volcanoes, [[Mount Ash]] spread so much fire, that it threatened to burn down the entire continent and everything in it. Only the combined efforts of Narre, Sopack, Orisia, and [[Steelan]] were able to push back the fires and save the continent. This event, know as [[The Great Fire]] lead to the last remaining Gods on Efthygra to fully retreat into the Heavens, thus ending the [[Divine Era]].

When [[The Chaos Wars]] began, Braruun saw this as an opportunity to unleash his creations. Therdrin had already become engaged in the war and Braruun convinced him that by giving life to his creations, he could help to avenge [[Tanyso]]. Of course, upon receiving Therdrin's aid, Braruun sent his creations to unleash terror against all of Efthygra, no matter who they fought for. Only his loyal followers, and those belonging to a species he had created were left alone. For awhile, Braruun gleefully engaged in the Wars, sowing destruction, until Steelan was imprisoned. Not wanting to suffer a similar fate, he retreated back to the Heavens.

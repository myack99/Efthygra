# Description
**FAERAS** is the Efthygran God of Magic. Faeras came into being in the year 6750 BR (1984 DE). After the first generations of [[Elf | Elves]] had long been gone, the current Elves became greedy. Not being satisfied with the purely [[Elemental Magic | Elemental]] accees they had to magic, they wanted to cast magic like the Gods. Seeing an opportunity, the God of Death, [[Morrom]] offered a prophecy to the Elves:

    "Nine Gods among the mortals be Divine
    With Magic they influence space and time
    This Magic, Elves across the lands have naught
    Until before them a proposal brought
    Bring to the God of Death a sacrifice
    A mortal soul for Morrom will suffice
    For each God in the Heavens or the Land
    An Elf must fall and be brought by one's hand
    If followed these instructions well and true
    A mortal with new Magic he'll imbue"

With this prophecy, an Elf named [[Magic Creation Elf | (Name TBD)]] murdered nine of his bretheren and brough them before Morrom. However, rather than grant Magic to the Elves, he sent his [[Divine Magic | Divine Power]] into one of the sacrifices and turned them into the God [[Navay]]. Navay, being born of decpetion, inherited deception and trickery as her domain; and what better trick, than to deceive her pwn purpose for existence. Navay cast off her own arm, filling it with the full might of her Divine Power, and thus Faeras was born.

Upon his creation, Faeras began working to bring Magic to the Elves. He tapped into the combined Divine Power within himself and the Elemental Powers of the world itself, weaving them into an accessible stream of Magic, known as the [[Amethyne Fabric of Magic |  Amethyne Fabric of Magic (The Amethyne)]]. Then plucking an Amethyst from a nearby cavern, he ssang into it. The Amethyst rang with the sound of Magic throughout Efthygra. By attuning with an Amethyst, any mortal could tap into the power of the Amethyne and wield magic themselves.

This new power, freely given to the Elves, angered the Gods [[Therdrin]] and [[Braruun]]. As a response, they forged the God [[Alusafa]] and began a rampage of sestruction agsinst the Elves. Faeras fought alongside [[Narre]], [[Steelan]], [[Navay]], [[Tanyso]], and [[Tanysa]] to protect the Elves. During this War, Faeras opened a new realm known as the [[Amethyne Realm]]. Here, some Elves were brought, to protect them from the War. These Elves became the [[Fae]]. Eventually, the other Gods grew tired of the War, and allowed the surviving mortals to remain.

When [[The Chaos Wars]] began, the Athalan Elves of [[Vandor Athala]][^1] called upon Faeras to protect them. Faeras descended upon the Kingdom of [[Athalamere]] and csat a protective spell across the [[Athalan Forest]]. Throughout the War, this Kingdom remained forgotten and untouched. Faeras did not become involved with the War, and instead reamined in Athalamere for its duration. At the end of the War, Faeras returned to the Heavens, however he left the spell intact, until the Elves decided to emerge from their solitude during the [[Re-Emergence]].

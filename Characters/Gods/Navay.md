# Description
**NAVAY** is the Efthygran Goddess of Trickery and Deceipt. Navay came into being in the year 9750 BR (1984 DE). After the first generations of [[Elf | Elves]] had long been gone, the current Elves became greedy. Not being satisfied with the purely [[Elemental Magic | Elemental]] accees they had to magic, they wanted to cast magic like the Gods. Seeing an opportunity, the God of Death, [[Morrom]] offered a prophecy to the Elves:

    "Nine Gods among the mortals be Divine
    With Magic they influence space and time
    This Magic, Elves across the lands have naught
    Until before them a proposal brought
    Bring to the God of Death a sacrifice
    A mortal soul for Morrom will suffice
    For each God in the Heavens or the Land
    An Elf must fall and be brought by one's hand
    If followed these instructions well and true
    A mortal with new Magic he'll imbue"

With this prophecy, an Elf named [[Magic Creation Elf | (Name TBD)]] murdered nine of his bretheren and brough them before Morrom. However, rather than grant Magic to the Elves, he sent his [[Divine Magic | Divine Power]] into one of the sacrifices and turned them into the God Navay. Navay, being born of decpetion, inherited deception and trickery as her domain; and what better trick, than to deceive her pwn purpose for existence. Navay cast off her own arm, filling it with the full might of her Divine Power, and thus [[Faeras]] was born.

With the creation of Faeras and Magic being given to the Elves, [[The First Divine War]] began. Navay fought alongside [[Narre]], Faeras, [[Steelan]], [[Tanyso]], and [[Tanysa]] to protect the mortals. During this War, Faeras opened a new realm known as the [[Amethyne Realm]]. Here, some Elves were brought, to protect them from the War. Upon creating the FaeRealm, a similar, yet opposite realm sprang into creation. Navay claimed control of this [[Umbra Realm]] and kidnapped a couple Elves, which eventually became the [[Shadow Elf | Shadow Elves]]. 

During [[The Chaos Wars]], Navay became involved, fighting in the shadows, often deceiving factions into fighting each other on false premises. She was pivotal in helping to imprison [[Steelan]].

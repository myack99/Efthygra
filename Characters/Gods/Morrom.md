# Description
**MORROM** is the Efthygran God of Death. Morrom came into being sometime between the years 9744 BR (5008 DE) and 6750 BR (1984 DE). After creating the [[Elf | Elves]], [[Narre]] created new Gods to assist them. Morrom was given the domain of Death, while [[Anchesena]] was given Knowledge, [[Tanyso]] was given the Sun and Light, and [[Tanysa]] the Moon and Darkness. While Anchsesena brought Knowledge to the [[Elf | Elves]], Morrom brought with this, the Knoweldge of their own mortality. Anchesena brought the gift of curiosity to the Elves and led them to explore Efthygra past the borders of the [[Athalan Forest]]. As they left the safety of thier birthplace, the first Elves experienced Death.

After the first generations of Elves had long been gone, the current Elves became greedy. Not being satisfied with the purely [[Elemental Magic | Elemental]] accees they had to magic, they wanted to cast magic like the Gods. Seeing an opportunity, the God of Death, Morrom offered a prophecy to the Elves:

    "Nine Gods among the mortals be Divine
    With Magic they influence space and time
    This Magic, Elves across the lands have naught
    Until before them a proposal brought
    Bring to the God of Death a sacrifice
    A mortal soul for Morrom will suffice
    For each God in the Heavens or the Land
    An Elf must fall and be brought by one's hand
    If followed these instructions well and true
    A mortal with new Magic he'll imbue"

With this prophecy, an Elf named [[Magic Creation Elf | (Name TBD)]] murdered nine of his bretheren and brough them before Morrom. However, rather than grant Magic to the Elves, he sent his [[Divine Magic | Divine Power]] into one of the sacrifices and turned them into the God [[Navay]]. Navay, being born of decpetion, inherited deception and trickery as her domain; and what better trick, than to deceive her pwn purpose for existence. Navay cast off her own arm, filling it with the full might of her Divine Power, and thus [[Faeras]] was born.

During [[The Chaos Wars]], Morrom rarely got directly involved with the fighting, however he very eagerly backed various factions. He thrived off of the Death in the War, which brought numerous souls under his possession. Like [[Alusafa]], he remained on Efthyga until the end of the War, though his direct involvement was minimal.

# Description
**STEELAN** is the Efthygran God of Fate and Fortune. Steelan came into being in the year 6750 BR (1984 DE). When the newly created God of Magic, [[Faeras]] granted magic to the [[Elf | Elves]] and some of the First Gods were angry. As a response, [[Braruun]] forged Alusafa out of stone and [[Therdrin]] breathed life into him. Alusafa was born with the sole purpose of enacting revenge on the Elves. This was the start of [[The First Divine War]]. During the War, [[Narre]] fought hard, alongside  [[Faeras]], [[Navay]], [[Tanyso]], and [[Tanysa]], to protect her creations, however she feared it was a losing battle. She created the Steelan, to be a shield to the Elves, granting them good fortune. Eventually, [[The First Divine War]] ended and Steelan's protection allowed the Elves reamined present on Efthygra. 

In 4766 BR (0 DE/ME), Braruun sent his power through the UnderCore out into the Earth he caused mountains to erupt into flames, creating the first volcanoes. One of these volcanoes, [[Mount Ash]] spread so much fire, that it threatened to burn down the entire continent and everything in it. Only the combined efforts of Steelan, [[Orisia]], Narre, and [[Sopack]] were able to push back the fires and save the continent. This event, know as [[The Great Fire]] lead to the last remaining Gods on Efthygra to fully retreat into the Heavens, thus ending the [[Divine Era]].

Shortly before the beginning of [[The Chaos Wars]], the former Cleric of [[Tanyso]], [[Rowan Stujice]], was approached by one of Steelan's followers. Slowly, Rowan starts to lean into the ideals of Steelan and becomes obsessed with the notions of fortune and misfortune. They become a devout worshipper of Steelan and begin to think of fortune and misfortune in terms of fate and revenge. 

Fastinated by their story and resolve, Steelan decides to speak to Rowan. As they continue to speak, Rowan convinces him that Tanyso must be fated for revenge and that Gods are not immune to fortune. Steelan grants them [[Divine Magic]] and sets them onto the path of  [[Aruza]]'s Governor, a devout worshipper of Tanyso, and also a corrupt politician. 

Sometime later, Rowan acquires the [[Orb of Day and Night]] and plans to use it to kill Tanyso and ascend in his place. Rowan takes their plan to Steelan and he becomes corrupted by their lust for revenge. In return for his help, Rowan promises to ascend in Tanyso's place, giving Steelan an ally, and thus improving his fortune. Thus Steelan begins setting out ot kill another God.

Rowan and their friends, [[Taron Crowe]] and [[Breah Crowe]] gather all of the members of the Church of Tanyso that are present in the city.  Rowan reveals the presence of the explosives and calls out to Tanyso, telling him that they will annihilate all of his followers. Rather than smite them on the spot, Tanyso appears in the church out of sheer curiosity of what their plan was now that they had his attention. At this point, Steelan appears and adds his powers to the magical seals on the building, preventing even Tanyso from teleporting out. He then reveals that the explosives are laced with a powerful magic, strong enough to kill a God[^2]. Tanyso rushes to engage Steelan, but Steelan teleports himself and Rowan out of the church and away from the radius of the explosion (though Steelan returns to the Heavens, while Rowan remains on Efthygra). Rowan than triggers the explosion, bringing down the church and killing everyone inside, including Tanyso. The smoke from the magical explosion fills the sky and does not dissipate, blacking out the Sun until years in the future, when [[Kaelahaas]] ascends as the new Sun God.

Unwilling to support their act of terrorism, the Crowes engage in a fight against Rowan. Rowan easily has the upper hand, but then [[Liaeyra Alusefeyre]] arrives and rescues the Crowes, overpowering Rowan. Rowan is wounded and at the mercy of Liaeyra, when Steelan teleports them away to safety. 

Rowan loses access to the [[Orb of Day and Night]], which they had stolen previously. [[The Chaos Wars]] begin immediately following Rowan's terrorist attack, thrusting all the Gods into action. Rowan leads a faction during this war with the sole aim to acquire the Orb and use it to ascend in Tanyso's place[^3]. Together Rowan and Steelan lead a major faction in the ongoing War.

At some point during the War, Rowan is killed and Steelan becomes imprisoned in th [[Stone of Banishment]]. This imprisonment marks a major turning point in the war, and causes most of the other Gods to retreat to the Heavens. 

Several thousand years later, the mage [[Pasifae]] attempts to release Steelan from his imprisonment.

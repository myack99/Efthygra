# Description
**ALUSAFA** is the Efthygran God of War. Alusafa came into being in the year 6750 BR (1984 DE). The newly created God [[Faeras]] had granted magic to the [[Elf | Elves]] and some of the First Gods were angry. As a response, [[Braruun]] forged Alusafa out of stone and [[Therdrin]] breathed life into him. Alusafa was born with the sole purpose of enacting revenge on the Elves. This was the start of [[The First Divine War]]. 

During the War, Alusafa and Braruun lead legions of Dark Creatures[^1] against Narre and her Elves. Therdrin backed Alusafa and Braruun, though he did not engage in the War as much. Eventually the other Gods grew tired of fighting and Alusafa backed away from the War until another opportunity would arise.

When [[The Chaos Wars]] began, Alusafa was one of the first Gods to get involved, rallying his followers to rampage across the world, killing all they could. Alusafa was personally rsponsible for much of the lasting destruction on Efthygra.

As in The First Divine War, Alusafa was once again the last God to retreat from The Chaos Wars. After [[Steelan]]'s imprisonment, all the others retreated to the Heavens while Alusafa continued his rampage. Eventually, he was defeated by an Elf named [[Matrias Mahrei]]. He retreated back to the Heavens in defeat and the remaining factions fell into disarray, effectively ending the War.

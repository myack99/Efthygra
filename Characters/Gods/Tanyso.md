# Description
**TANYSO** was the Efthygan God of The Sun and Light. Tanysa came into being sometime between the years 9744 BR (5008 DE) and 6750 BR (1984 DE). After creating the [[Elf | Elves]], [[Narre]] created new Gods to assist them. Tanyso was given the domain of the Sun and Light, while [[Tanysa]] was given the Moon and Darkness, [[Morrom]] was given Death, and [[Anchesena]] was given Knowledge. 

While Anchsena and Morrom were created separately, Tanyso and Tanysa sprung from the same tree. Thus the two were twin Gods, one of light and one of darkness. Tanyso and Tanysa hated each other and were known for their intense rivalry.

With the creation of [[Faeras]] and Magic being given to the Elves, [[The First Divine War]] began. Tanysa fought alongside [[Narre]], Faeras, [[Steelan]], [[Navay]], and relucantatly, Tanysa, to protect the mortals. Eventually, [[The First Divine War]] ended and the Elves reamined present of Efthygra. 

Shortly before th start of [[The Chaos Wars]], one of Tanyso's most dedicated followers, the cleric [[Rowan Stujice]] encountered a group of [[Followers of Tanysa]]. After tailing the Tanysans with their friends [[Finn Ferus]] and [[Brenna Blackstarr]], both groups become trapped together. Reluctantly they work together and eventually come to trust each other. During their escape, several members of both groups fall, inlcuding the Tanysan Cleric, [[Yandular Mazzaejuil]]. Rowan then tries to step in as a healer for both groups, however Tanyso does not allow it. Rowan, being a mage of [[Divine Magic]], is unable to cast without being permitted by Tanyso. He blocks any attempts by Rowan to heal the Followers of Tanysa, causing them to die.

As Rowan and their now smaller group escape with the [[Orb of Day and Night]], Rowan goes to confront Tanyso. Tanyso reveals that he blocked their magic, in order to deny aid to anyone who follows his sworn enemy, Tanysa. Rowan is apalled by this information and challenges Tanyso. Tanyso simply takes away their [[Divine Magic]] and leaves. 

Several months later, Rowan gathers all of Tanyso's followers in the city of [[Aruza]] and reveals the presence of the explosives, calling out to Tanyso, telling him that they will annihilate all of his followers. Rather than smite them on the spot, Tanyso appears in the church out of sheer curiosity of what their plan was now that they had his attention. At this point, Steelan appears and adds his powers to the magical seals on the building, preventing even Tanyso from teleporting out. He then reveals that the explosives are laced with a powerful magic, strong enough to kill a God. Tanyso rushes to engage Steelan, but Steelan teleports himself and Rowan out of the church and away from the radius of the explosion (though Steelan returns to the Heavens, while Rowan remains on Efthygra). Rowan than triggers the explosion, bringing down the church and killing everyone inside, including Tanyso. The smoke from the magical explosion fills the sky and does not dissipate, blacking out the Sun until years in the future, when [[Kaelahaas]] ascends as the new Sun God. Tanyso's death marks the start of [[The Chaos Wars]].

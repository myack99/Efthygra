# Description
**TANYSA** is the Efthygan Goddess of The Moon and Darkness. Tanysa came into being sometime between the years 9744 BR (5008 DE) and 6750 BR (1984 DE). After creating the [[Elf | Elves]], [[Narre]] created new Gods to assist them. Tanysa was given the domain of the Moon and Darkness, while [[Tanyso]] was given the Sun and Light, [[Morrom]] was given Death, and [[Anchesena]] was given Knowledge. 

While Anchsena and Morrom were created separately, Tanysa and Tanyso sprung from the same tree. Thus the two were twin Gods, one of light and one of darkness. Tanysa and Tanyso hated each other and were known for their intense rivalry.

With the creation of [[Faeras]] and Magic being given to the Elves, [[The First Divine War]] began. Tanysa fought alongside [[Narre]], Faeras, [[Steelan]], [[Navay]], and relucantatly, Tanyso, to protect the mortals. Eventually, [[The First Divine War]] ended and the Elves reamined present of Efthygra. 

When [[The Chaos Wars]] began, Tanysa was stunned. While she and Tanyso had been rivals, they were still siblings. And if Tanyso could die, so could she. Tanysa joined the War against any followers of Tanyso, however she used this as a guise to seek out how he had been killed. Upon discovering that the [[Orb of Day and Night]] was responsible for his death, she stole it away to the Heavens and retreated from the War.

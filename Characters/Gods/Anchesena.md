# Description
**ANCHESENA** is the Efthygran Goddess of Wisdom. Anchesena came into being sometime between the years 9744 BR (5008 DE) and 6750 BR (1984 DE). After creating the [[Elf | Elves]], [[Narre]] created new Gods to assist them. Anchesena was given the domain of Knowledge, while [[Morrom]] was given Death, [[Tanyso]] was given the Sun and Light, and [[Tanysa]] the Moon and Darkness. Anchesena brought the gift of Knoweldge and curiosity to the Elves and led them to explore Efthygra past the borders of the [[Athalan Forest]].

When [[Alusafa]] began [[The First Divine War]] against the Elves, Achesena remained neutral. She hoped to see how the Elves faired with the gifts she had given them and was pleased with herself when they survived the onslaught.

When [[The Chaos Wars]] began, Anchesena was one of the last Gods to get involved. Eventually however, a group of scholars, interested in studying the different factions in order to determine their motives, grabbed her attention. She joined the War and worked to ascertain the truths and mysteries of the War. This group was pivotal in learning how to imprison the God [[Steelan]]. Following his capture, Anchesena withdrew to the Heavens alongside the other Gods.

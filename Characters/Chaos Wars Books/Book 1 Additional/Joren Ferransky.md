# Description
**JOREN FERRANSKY** was a Wood Elf, devoted to the Sun God, [[Tanyso]]. During his time as a member of the [[Followers of Tanyso]], he became close friends with [[Rowan Stujice]], [[Finn Ferus]], and [[Brenna Blackstarr]]. Shortly before the start of [[The Chaos Wars]], Joren and his friends were gathered in a marketplace in [[Aruza]]. A group of [[Followers of Tanysa]], led by [[Albrecht Elmwood]] discover these friends and one member, [[Dorian Strongman]] approaches them and starts a fight, and kills Joren.

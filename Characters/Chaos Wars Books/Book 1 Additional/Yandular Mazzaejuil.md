# Description
**YANDULAR MAZZAEJUIL** was a Half-Elf that spent his life devoted to the Moon Goddess, [[Tanysa]]. He joined a group known as the [[Followers of Tanysa]].
Shortly before the [[The Chaos Wars]] began, Yandular joined [[Albrecht Elmwood]]'s group, tasked with retrieving the [[Orb of Day and Night]],which has been located in the city of  [[Aruza]][^1]. They discover where the artifact is being held, however they also find a small group of [[Followers of Tanyso]] (which includes [[Rowan Stujice]]).
The two groups briefly fight, before realizing that this artifact was part of a trap set[^2] for the followers of both Gods. They realize that they must work together in order to survive.
However, before they are able to escape, Yandular is killed.

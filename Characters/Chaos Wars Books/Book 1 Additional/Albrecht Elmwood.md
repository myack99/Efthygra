# Description
**ALBRECHT ELMWOOD** was an [[Custom Amphibious Race TBD]] that spent his life devoted to the Moon Goddess, [[Tanysa]]. He joined a group known as the [[Followers of Tanysa]].
Shortly before the [[The Chaos Wars]] began, Albrecht was tasked with leading a group to retrieve the [[Orb of Day and Night]],which has been located in the city of  [[Aruza]][^1]. Leading his team, they discover where the artifact is being held, however they also find a small group of [[Followers of Tanyso]] (which includes [[Rowan Stujice]]).
The two groups briefly fight, before realizing that this artifact was part of a trap set[^2] for the followers of both Gods. They realize that they must work together in order to survive.
However, before they are able to escape, Albrecht is severely wounded. Rowan attempts to heal him, but has their healing powers blocked by [[Tanyso]]. Without help, Albrecht dies.

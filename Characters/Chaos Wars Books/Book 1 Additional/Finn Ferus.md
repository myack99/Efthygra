# Description
**FINN FERUS** was an Aarakocra Air Genasi, devoted to the Sun God, [[Tanyso]]. During his time as a member of the [[Followers of Tanyso]], he became close friends with [[Rowan Stujice]], [[Brenna Blackstarr]], and [[Joren Ferransky]]. Shortly before the start of [[The Chaos Wars]], Finn and his friends were gathered in a marketplace in [[Aruza]]. A group of [[Followers of Tanysa]], led by [[Albrecht Elmwood]] discover these friends and one member, [[Dorian Strongman]] approaches them and starts a fight, resulting in the deaths of both Joren and Dorian. Finn and his friends track down the rest of Albrecht's group, and end up being forced to work together to survive. Finn escapes from this event with Rowan. Later, Finn remains clsoe with Rowan, despite their drifting away from Tanyso. Eventually, Finn discovers what Rowan is up to and confronts them, culminating in epic back that results in Finn's death at Rowan's hands.

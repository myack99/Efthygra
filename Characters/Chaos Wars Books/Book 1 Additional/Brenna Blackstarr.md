# Description
**BRENNA BLACKSTAR** was a Mountain Dwarf, devoted to the Sun God, [[Tanyso]]. During her time as a member of the [[Followers of Tanyso]], she became close friends with [[Rowan Stujice]], [[Finn Ferus]], and [[Joren Ferransky]]. Shortly before the start of [[The Chaos Wars]], Brenna and her friends were gathered in a marketplace in [[Aruza]]. A group of [[Followers of Tanysa]], led by [[Albrecht Elmwood]] discover these friends and one member, [[Dorian Strongman]] approaches them and starts a fight, resulting in the deaths of both Joren and Dorian. Brenna and her friends track down the rest of Albrecht's group, and end up being forced to work together to survive. During this time, Brenna is mortally wounded. Rowan, used the [[Divine Magic]] granted to them by Tanyso to heal Brenna, but it unable to heal any of [[Tanysa]]'s followers. Brenna escapes from this event with Rowan. Brenna is later killed by Rowan after they turn to [[Steelan]] and become corrupted with revenge.

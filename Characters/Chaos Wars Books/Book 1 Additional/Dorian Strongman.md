# Description
**DORIAN STRONGMAN** was a Half-Orc that spent his life devoted to the Moon Goddess, [[Tanysa]]. He joined a group known as the [[Followers of Tanysa]].
Shortly before the [[The Chaos Wars]] began, Dorian joined [[Albrecht Elmwood]]'s group, tasked with retrieving the [[Orb of Day and Night]],which has been located in the city of  [[Aruza]][^1]. 
While scouting the city, [[Bea Liza]] discovers a small group of [[Followers of Tanyso]] (which includes [[Rowan Stujice]]). Since they appear to not be on the trail of the Orb, the group elects to ignore them. However, Dorian splits off from the group and approaches Rowan's group. He confronts them and kills Rowan's friend, [[Joren Ferransky]]. This angers Rowan and their other friends, who in turn kill Dorian.

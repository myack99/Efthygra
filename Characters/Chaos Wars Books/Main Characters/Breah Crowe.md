# Description
**BREAH CROWE** was a wizard that studied in the city of [[Aruza]] prior to the [[The Chaos Wars]]. While she believed in using magic for great things, she did often work assist her brother, [[Taron Crowe]], by creating illusions during his Bardic performances in taverns. Upon meeting [[Rowan Stujice]], the two develop feelings for each other, and briefly enter into a casually romantic relationship. Breah and Taron assist Rowan in their quest to expose the corrupt Governor of Koilla[^1]. Being born into a wealthy family, they are able to use their connections and influence to assist in finding information, in ways that Rowan would have been unable to. However, when Rowan reveals their plan to kill the God [[Tanyso]] and all of his followers in the church, Breah and Taron are repulsed and try to stop her. They are only saved by the Fae, [[Liaeyra Alusefeyre]], whom distrusted Rowan and was therefore pushed away by the group.

After the Chaos Wars start, Breah fights alongside Taron and Liaeyra to stop Rowan. At some point, she meets the pirate [[Isabella from DA2]] and falls in love.

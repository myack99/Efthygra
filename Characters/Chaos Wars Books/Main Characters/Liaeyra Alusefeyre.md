# Description
**LIAEYRA ALUSEFEYRE** was a Fae that was shunned from her tribe in the [[Amethyne Realm]] because of her use of [[Blood Magic]]. She sought refuge in [[Efthygra]], where she met [[Taron Crowe]], [[Breah Crowe]], and [[Rowan Stujice]] in the city of [[Aruza]]. She distrusted Rowan, and tension grew between them, pushing her away from the group. The only thing keeping her from abandoning them was Taron, whom she had developed feelings for. The two eventually fell in love. During an argument with Rowan, Liaeyra leaves the group, shortly before Rowan destroys the Church of [[Tanyso]]. While Rowan, Taron, and Breah go about their plan, Liaeyra breaks into Rowan's residence to look for any suspicious evidence. While snooping around, she discovers the [[Orb of Day and Night]], just as a massive explosion shakes the city. Liaeyra sees that this explosion came from the direction of the Church of Tanyso. She pockets the Orb and rushes off to save her friends. Upon arriving, she sees that Taron and Breah are engaged in a fight against Rowan, and they are losing. Liaeyra rushes in and pushes off Rowan until Rowan suddenly teleports away.

Liaeyra then goes on to be a major general during [[The Chaos Wars]].

Several years into the Chaos Wars, her husband, Taron is killed in battle. Liaeyra attempts to help his prtoege, [[Matrias Mahrei]], who eventually goes on to end the War. 

With the war over, there are still many problems to solve and difficulties to face. Liaeyra returns to the FaeRealm, hoping to gain insight into where [[Faeras]] was during the War and to try to seek out a way to restore the light.

When [[Kaelahaas LastName]] and the other Athalan Elves emerge from [[Athalamere]], Liaeyra serves as an advisor to Kaelahaas, assisting him in his campaign to bring the light back to Efthygra.

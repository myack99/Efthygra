# Description
**TARON CROWE** was a Human Bard, born to wealthy parents. Despite his parents being government officials, Taron preferred to spend his time drinking and performing in bars and taverns throughout [[Aruza]][^1]. 

One day while performing, he meets [[Rowan Stujice]], who is depresssed and drinking away their sorrows after being dumped by their God, [[Tanyso]]. Taron and Rowan become friends, and he eventually introduces them to his sister, [[Breah Crowe]]. Breah and Taron assist Rowan in their quest to expose the corrupt Governor of Koilla[^2]. Being born into a wealthy family, they are able to use their connections and influence to assist in finding information, in ways that Rowan would have been unable to. 

During thier investigation, the trio encounters a Fae named [[Liaeyra Alusefeyre]]. Taron is immediately smitten with her and the two fall in love. Rowan and Breah distrust Liaeyra, however Taron sees past her use of [[Blood Magic]] and remains devoted to her. This love keeps Liaeyra around.

Taron, Breah, and Rowan successfully gather all of the members of the Church of Tanyso that are present in the city, in order to expose the governor's corruption. Rowan then reveals reveals their plan to kill the God [[Tanyso]] and all of his followers in the church, Breah and Taron are repulsed and try to stop her. Rowan has the upper had until Liaeyra arrives to save them.

Taron goes on to lead a major faction in [[The Chaos Wars]], fighting to stop Rowan and their followers. Taron is eventually responsible for bringing down Rowan, however the war still continues.

Years later, Taron recruits a younf elf named [[Matrias Mahrei]] to help join the cause. He takes Matrias under his wing until he is killed in battle. Matrias then carries on Taron's legacy and ends the war.

# Description
**KAELAHAS LASTNAME** was a High Elf of [[Vandor Athala]]. During [[The Chaos Wars]], Kaelahaas and the other Athalan Elves reamined safely tucked away from  the rest of the world, under the magical protection of [[Faeras]]. After years of the rest of Efthygra being in a [[Dark Age]], Kaelahaas convinced the Athalan Elves to emerge from [[Athalamere]] and bring light back to Efthygra. He leads a group across the continent of [[Chorvas]] and restores the various Kingdoms, uniting them under the Kingdom [[Neofos (Kingdom)]]. 

Kaelahaas ascends to Godhood, becoming the new God of the Sun, in place of the Slain God [[Tanyso]].

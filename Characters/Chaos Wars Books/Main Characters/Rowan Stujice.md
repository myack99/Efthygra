# Description
**ROWAN STUJICE** was a Satyr worshipper of the Sun God [[Tanyso]]. They were a devout follower and had been granted [[Divine Magic]] from him. At some point, Rowan was in the city of [[Aruza]] with their friends, [[Finn Ferus]], [[Joren Ferransky]], and [[Brenna Blackstarr]], when they were attacked by a [[Followers of Tanysa | Follower of Tanysa]] named [[Dorian Strongman]]. Dorian killed Joren and in a rage, Rowan in turn, killed Dorian. Rowan and their friends then went to track down the rest of Dorian's group and discover a quest being led by [[Albrecht Elmwood]] to retrieve the [[Orb of Day and Night]], which was a powerful artifact, sacred to both Tanyso and [[Tanysa]]. Rowan and their friends seek to reach the Orb first.

The location of the Orb, however, had been revealed as a way to lure followers of both Tanysa and Tanyso into a trap. The two groups become forced to work together in order to survive. At some point, the Tanysan group's healer, [[Yandular Mazzaejuil]] is killed. With Rowan now being the only healer, they attempt to heal the wounds of everyone else. Their magic, however, only works on [[Brenna Blackstarr]]. All of the Tanysan followers, are unable to be healed.

Upon claiming the Orb and escaping, Rowan heads to the Church of Tanyso to confront their God about why their magic failed. It is revealed that Tanyso blocked their magic, in order to deny aid to anyone who follows his sworn enemy, Tanysa. Rowan is apalled by this information and challenges Tanyso. Tanyso simply takes away their [[Divine Magic]] and leaves. 

Now cast about by their God and left powerless, Rowan heads to a local tavern to drink away their sorrows. Here, a Steelanite Priest approaches them and tells them to come visit the Church of [[Steelan]] to gain fortune. Rowan dismisses this priest and returns home to the Church of Tanyso, where they encouter Finn. The two of them get into an argument about Tanyso and Rowan storms off. They decide that with nowhere to go, maybe they should check out this Steelanite Church and maybe get some closure. 

Slowly, Rowan starts to lean into the ideals of Steelan and becomes obsessed with the notions of fortune and misfortune. They become a devout worshipper of Steelan and begin to think of fortune and misfortune in terms of fate and revenge. 

This new religious devotion does not do anything to cure their depression and Rowan continues to frequent the taverns, getting themselves drunk. At one of these taverns, [[Taron Crowe]] is a regular performer. Rowan talks to him often and they develop a sort of friendship. They also develop a crush on Taron's younger sister [[Breah Crowe]]. They start to hang out with the brother-sister duo and begin to get their life back on track.

Eventually, Rowan makes contact with Steelan and convinces him that Tanyso must be fated for revenge and that Gods are not immune to fortune. Steelan grants them [[Divine Magic]] and sets them onto the path of  [[Aruza]]'s Governor, a devout worshipper of Tanyso, and also a corrupt politician. Rowan turns to their newfound friends, the Crowes, whom have family connections to the high ranking members of the government. They use the angle of stopping corruption to gain Taron and Breah's assistance. Rowan also furthers their relationship with Breah in order to further gain her trust.

During their investigations, the trio come across a Fae [[Liaeyra Alusefeyre]]. Liaeyra and Rowan are both immediately suspicious of each other and Rowan realizes that they need to find a way to get rid of her. This proves difficult though, as Taron and Liaeyra develop feelings for each other and the resulting relationship keeps Liaeyra from abaondoning the group entirely. Rowan keeps trying to find ways to push her out, and eventually discovers that Liaeyra is a [[Blood magic | Blood Mage]], which is often considered taboo. Rowan uses this to push Liaeyra away further. 

While investigating the corruption of the governor, Rowan continues to seek their revenge against other [[Followers of Tanyso]], hoping to get back at Tanyso. They openly protest and besmirch Tanyso in the streets and steal and sabotage shipments. [[Finn Ferus]], who had remained friends with Rowan throughout all of this, takes notice of this and they have a huge fallout. As they continue with this revenge, sometimes, people are inadvertently killed. Eventually, Rowan decides that they must steal back the [[Orb of Day and Night]]. While attempting to do this, they set off explosives that inadvertently kill their old friend [[Brenna Blackstarr]]. Rowan is not happy about this and decides to blme Tanyso for Brenna's death, convincing themself that none of this would have happened if Tanyso didn't exist. At this point, Rowan decides that the only way to end this is to kill Tanyso

Rowan takes their plan to Steelan and convinces him to help them kill a god. In return, Rowan promises to ascend in Tanyso's place, giving Steelan an ally, and thus improving his fortune.

Rowan sets out with their plan to kill Tanyso by luring him to his temple with the threat of killing all of his followers. In order to get all of his followers gathered in one place, Rowan, Taron, and Breah plan to expose the corruption of the governor in front of all of his fellow Tanysoan worshippers. Liaeyra thinks this is a terrible idea and fight them on it, but she gets rejected, so Liaeyra leaves the group to carry out this plan on their own.

Rowan, Breah, and Taron successfully gather all of the members of the Church of Tanyso that are present in the city. While Breah and Taron expose the governor's corruption to the crowds, Rowan sneaks away to plant the explosives around the church. While doing this, they are caught by [[Finn Ferus]]. Finn begs Rowan to stop, but they refuse and a fight ensues. While Finn is more powerful the Rowan, his reluctance to kill her causes him to hesitate, giving Rowan the upper hand. They kill Finn and then place the explosives.

Upon returning back to the gathered Tanysoan worshippers, Rowan removes[^1] Taron and Breah from the premises and then magically seals everyone inside. Rowan reveals the presence of the explosives and calls out to Tanyso, telling him that they will annihilate all of his followers. Rather than smite them on the spot, Tanyso appears in the church out of sheer curiosity of what their plan was now that they had his attention. At this point, Steelan appears and adds his powers to the magical seals on the building, preventing even Tanyso from teleporting out. He then reveals that the explosives are laced with a powerful magic, strong enough to kill a God[^2]. Tanyso rushes to engage Steelan, but Steelan teleports himself and Rowan out of the church and away from the radius of the explosion (though Steelan returns to the Heavens, while Rowan remains on Efthygra). Rowan than triggers the explosion, bringing down the church and killing everyone inside, including Tanyso. The smoke from the magical explosion fills the sky and does not dissipate, blacking out the Sun until years in the future, when [[Kaelahaas]] ascends as the new Sun God.

Safely outside of explosion, Taron and Breah confront Rowan. Unwilling to support their act of terrorism, the Crowes engage in a fight against Rowan. Rowan easily has the upper hand, but then Liaeyra arrives and rescues the Crowes, overpowering Rowan. Rowan is wounded and at the mercy of Liaeyra, when Steelan teleports them away to safety. 

Rowan loses access to the [[Orb of Day and Night]], which they had stolen previously. [[The Chaos Wars]] begin immediately following Rowan's terrorist attack, thrusting all the Gods into action. Rowan leads a faction during this war with the sole aim to acquire the Orb and use it to ascend in Tanyso's place[^3]. The Orb however, was acquired by Liaeyra, who upon being sent away from the group prior to the bombing, investigated Rowan's home in ordder to investigate any suspicions. Liaeyra found the Orb and was inspecting it when the explosion went off. Liaeyra stashed the Orb and then went to rescue her friends.

During the War, Rowan is eventually killed[^4].

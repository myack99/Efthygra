# Notes of the major crime syndicates:

---

* [[Vermillion Dusk]] ruled by a Queen of the hells (Lady)
* [[White Crescent]] ruled by royal family of [[Crescencia]] (Prince)
* [[Cinderuga Clan]] ruled by royal family of [[Dokartol (Kingdom) | Dokartol]] (Queen)
* [[The Buuns]] ruled by leader of a nomadic tribe (Lord)
* [[Spyr Syndicate]] are just straight up a criminal organization

The major crime syndicates are mostly based in Chorvas. There are none in Toallo because they are strict on crime there, so no group is able to gain dominion outside of their primary city of operation. So they have localized crime, but not organized crime. The Tegem Isles have crime organizations, but the Isles are very elegiste and think that they are the center of society. So it’s rare for them to have much relations or interactions outside of the isles, with the exception of trade. Because of this, their major crime syndicates are entirely localized, and not worldwide. I do need to create some syndicates specific to the isles though. Dokartol is very much ruled entirely by the cinder clan. Because this syndicate is the royal family of the entire continent, there is no room for other syndicates to form, and any attempts are squashed. Most of their crime is localized, to the continent, but they are known to branch out occasionally. In Chorvas, the Bunns are a nomadic people, but they often reside within the Buun Gallows. This is also where the Lord Buun Father’s palace is. (These guys are inspired by the Hutts, but I guess I can kinda place them into this context similar to the Wildlings from GoT, where the Buun Gallows are where the King Beyond the Wall builds his army.) The Spyrs head of operations is located in the western kingdom (still to be named), in the same city as Stumbleduck Timbers’ shop. VD operates mainly out of Neofos, but their true HQ is in the Hells. Their crime business mainly operates in Efthygr, but they do do some business in the Hells. I also need some syndicates based in the Under Core.

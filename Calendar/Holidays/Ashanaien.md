# Description
**ASHANAIEN** is a  celebration of the New Year.
# History
Prior to the [[Re-Emergence]] (BR), this holiday was celebrateds on 1 [[Iquanoresh]], [[Kordaecath]]. However, during the Re-Emergence, the calendar year was restructured to make [[Tacoo]] the first month, thus relocating this holiday to 1 [[Iquanoresh]], [[Tacoo]].
# Celebration
Ashanaien is often celebrated with a large feast and lots of drinking.

# Description
**PELLINTESHAL ARSURENIAL** is a holiday that occurs every 420 days, when the Heavens eerupt with a gorgeous display of lights. Celebrations are held all throughout Efthygra, but the most spectacular view (and celebration) occurs in the [[Northern Mountains]]
# History
This Heavenly event first occurred the day that Calahas ascended to God-hood (1 AR). It has continued to occur every 420 days since.
# Celebration
Celebrations are held all throughout Efthygra, but the most spectacular view (and celebration) occurs in the [[Northern Mountains]]. Here, a massive festival is held where people from all over gather to dace, eat, drink, and gaze at the sky.

# Description
**ANSRIVARR** was established After the [[Re-Emergence]] as a Day of Remembrance for the [[Dark Age]]. This holiday is honored by gathering together in the dark for a candlelit moment of silence. Over time, more jubilant traditions were developed and it became a less solemn celebration. It is no longer as serious as it once was.

# Description
**THE GREAT FIRE** was an event where [[Braruun]] caused volcanoes to erupt all over [[Efthygra]]. It marked the end of the Divine Era and the start of the Mortal Era.

While Narre continued to create life, Braruun delved into creation of his own. He however, lacked the ability to breathe life into his creations. Growing angry at these circumstances, he finally snapped and tried to burn down all of Narre’s creations. Starting with the eruption of Mount Ash, his fire burned down nearly an entire continent before the collective efforts of Narre, Sopack, and Steelan were able to stop him. Braruun then retreated back into isolation, however he would be occasionally visited by Therdrin, who would breathe life into his creations. The efforts exerted in stopping Braruun, however, also drained Narre, and she stopped creating, retreating instead to the Outer Planes.

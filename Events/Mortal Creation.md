# Description
**MORTAL CREATION** is the expanse of almost 5000 years, in which mortal life was created on [[Efthygra]].

The gods then created one of their own, Narre (Life). With the help of Narre, the gods filled Efthygra with their creations and created the first sentient mortals: the elves. As the elves grew, the gods created more of their own. Morrom (Death), Anchesena (Knowledge), Tanyso (Harvest), and Tanysa (Hunt).
Around the year 11750 BR, the generation of original elves had been gone for a few hundred years and the new generations became greedy. They wanted to use magic, like the gods. The gods denied their requests. Morrom however devised a plan and told the elves that for every 9 elves sacrificed (one for each of the gods), he would grant magic to a single soul. One elf then brought him 9 sacrifices and in return, Morrom placed the soul of a new god into one of the corpses, technically delivering on his promise. This new god was Navay (Deceit and Chaos). She was ordered to kill the elf that brought the sacrifices, but being born of deceit, she deceived her maker, and cut off her own arm, forming the god Faeras (Magic). Faeras wove the energies of the planes into a fabric that could be accessed by mortals. Enraged by the fact that mortals could now access magic, Therdrin and Braruun forged the god Alusafa (War) out of stone and used him to punish the elves. Of course, Narre cared for creations, and did not want them to become extinct. She imbued part of her soul into an elf, thus creating the god Steelan (Fortune), hoping he would bring protection and good fortune to the elves. She also, however, created a new race, the gnomes, hoping that if the elves fell, the gnomes would at least be small enough to hide from the wrath of the other gods. The elves did not perish though, and eventually the gods withdrew their wrath, retreating to their respective domains. Narre continued to create life for the next 2000 years, bringing many new species and races to Efthygra.

# Description
**THE ALIGNMENT** was the creation of the world of [[Efthygra]].

---

    It has not been checked for changes in the lore.
    There may be some inaccuracies.

When all 9 of the Outer Planes aligned to converge in one spot, this spot being the intersection of the four Elemental Planes, the concentration of energy caused an implosion that formed a new sphere within the Material Plane. While new worlds were formed regularly within the expanse of the Material, this near impossible phenomenon caused a surge in magic power, unlike any other world before, causing the Planes themselves to temporarily phase into a new state. From the energy, four gods arose: Therdrin (Air), Sopack (Water), Orisia (Earth), and Braruun (Fire). The four gods created the world of Efthygra.

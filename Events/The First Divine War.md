# Description
**THE FIRST DIVINE WAR** was the War fought due Faeras bringing Magic to the Elves.

When Faeras brought Magic to the Elves, Braruun and Alusafa began a War against Narre and her creations. The two sides consisted of Narre, Steelan, Faeras, Navay, Tanyso, and Tanysa versus Braruun, Therdrin, and Alusafa. The other 4 gods stayed out of it. During this War, Braruun kidnapped a group of elves and took them to his domain in the Under Core. These Elves became the Dark Elves. Navay also created the first non-elven race: the gnomes. In an attempt to help protect some of the elves, Faeras opened a door to the FaeWild and took some elves there, These elves became the Hidden Elves (Eladrin). In opening the FaeWild, the energies of Efthygra also created an opening to the ShadowRealm, ts opposite. Navay claimed domain over this Realm.

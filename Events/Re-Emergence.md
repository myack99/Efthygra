# Description
**The RE-EMERGECNE** was the event that occured when the [[Elf | Elves]] of [[Athalamere]] emerged from their sanctuary in order to restore light to [[Efthygra]] after [[The Chaos Wars]] and the [[Dark Age]].

The Elves of Athalamere emerged from their forest and brought light back to the world of Efthygra. They created a new calendar and began a new world order. Many were grateful for the help of the elves, however many also despised the elves for hiding within their forest during the war. With the emergence of the elves, cities were rebuilt and countries were reformed. The elves began to spread across the lands, allowing themselves to find homes in other places outside of Athalamere. The ones that remained, were the religiously devout. Overtime, they became forgotten, and the forest became a place of mystery shrouded in magic.

# Description
**KOILLA** was a Republic State present on the continent of [[Chorvas]] prior to start of the [[The Chaos Wars]]. It was a member of the [[Chorvan Union]]. It's Capitol City was [[Aruza]].

# Districts
## [[Aruza County]]
Located in the Northeast of Koilla, Aruza County was the section of Koilla immediately South of the [[Northern Mountains]]. It boasted the end of the [[Warlan Fjord]] and Koilla's largest city, [[Aruza]].

# Geography
Occupying the [[Aruzan Valley]], Koilla was situated between the [[Northern Mountains]] and the [[Nevalene Montains]]. A small section of the Northern Mountins was also within Koilla's borders.

## Bodies of Water
The most notable bodies of water in Koilla are the [[Warlan Fjord]] and [[Avar Lake]]. There are also several other streams that feed into the [[Aruzan Valley]] from the bordering montain ranges:
* [[Belik River]]
* [[Dharb Creek]]
* [[Jallein Creek]]
* [[Kethlon Creek]]
* [[Kolik River]]
* [[Limitiko River]]
* [[Limvoku River]]
* [[Nellaren Stream]]
* [[Veltik River]]
* [[Xyntreas Creek]]
* [[Yvar River]]

# Description
**ARUZA** was a large city located in the th e Northernmost section of the Country of Koilla.

Nestled at the intersection of the [[Northern Mountains]] and the [[Nevalene Mountains]], Aruza lay safely tucked away on the far end of the [[Aruzan Valley]]. 

# City Districts
## Central Plaza
The Central Plaza of Aruza was a roughly 3mile by 2 mile section in the center o the city that was a popular destination for people throughout the day (both tourists and local).

The top of the plaza was a 2 mile radius circular cobblestone plaza with a variety of shops, restaurants, stages, and open space. South of this, a large grassy park made up the remainder of the plaza.

## Federal District
The Federal District of Aruza was located in the the Northeast of the city and housed all of the governmental buildings.

## Holy District
The Holy District of Aruza was located in the Northwest of the city and housed all of the churches and other reliigous buildings. 

## Mining District
The Mining District of Aruza was located at the northernmost part of the [[Nevalene Mountains]] and was separated from the rest of the city. It laid east of the Federal District.

## Tonamoye District[^1]
The Tonamoye District of Aruza was a long district that stretched from the Eastern side of the Central Plaza, al the way to the Eastern Nevalene Mountains on the eastern side of the city. Many businesses and corporations owned property here, though there were also plenty of residential buildings here as well.

## Warlan Valley District
The Warlan Valley District of Aruza was the largest part of the city, located to the Southeast and was where a majority of the population lived. This district extends all the way to the southern end of the city, where it meets the [[Warlan Fjord]] and the [[Resso Woods]]. 

This distrcit is subdivded into smaller communities:
### Asdon
Located in the northwest of the Warlan Valley District.

### Ethelsville
Located in the midwest of the Warlan Valley District.

### Riverside
Located along the [[Warlan Fjord]], Riverside is a small community in the southwest of the Warlan Valley District.

### Samo
Located in the northeast of the Warlan Valley District, this community is the poorest area in Aruza.

### Vanno
Located in the southeast of the Warlan Valley District, this community is less populated and is almost more suburban than urban.

## West Hills District
The West Hills District is located on the western side of the city and is the wealthiest area of the city.

# Hovertrains
The primary ways to traverse the city were either by foot, or aboard the various [[hovertrains]].

Hovertrains worked by using Amethysts to propel trains through rings, held up by large posts. Both the rings and the trains emitted a purple glow as the magic generated by the Amethysts in both components propelled the trains forward.

These rings, commonly refered to as  [[Hover Posts]] consisted of one large metal pole hundreds of feet tall. At the top of the pole, were two large circles, sitting sided by side, on opposite sides of the pole. Hovertrains would pass through these circles (one for each direction) as they moved across their routes.

While the hovertrains did work by magically hocvering and being propelled forward by this magic, there was still a thin tube/track that connected each Hover Post. These tracks only purpose was to guide the hovertrains to the next post, and did nothing to support the train itself.

Due to the nature of using [[Amethyne Magic]] to operate, both the Hover Posts and Hovertrains glowed purple. However, in Aruza, each was also equipped with a specifically colored light to denote which route they belonged to.

## Major Stations
There were many stations locateds throughout Aruza, where passengers could board, depart, or switch trains. Many of these stations were located along the smaller paths (Ways) and are considred Lesser Stations. However, all the stations along the larger paths (Loops) were considered Major Stations. Some Major Stations services multiple Loops.

### Plaza Station
**Loop Service: Blue | Green | Red**

Located on the southern edge of the Central Plaza's park, this station serviced three of the four major Loops in Aruza. It was the largest and busiest station in the city. Plaza Station also housed [[The Gate]], a large portal that allowed for quick travel in and out of the city.

### Altar Station
**Loop Service: Yellow**

Located at the north of the Upper Loop, this station is the smallest of the Major Stations.

### Corporate Station
**Loop Service: Red**

Located inside one of the mountains within the [[Nevalene Mountains | Nevalene Mountain Range]], this station served as the main hub for the Central Loop. All Pink Ways started at this station.

### Cross Station
**Loop Service: Blue**

Located at the south end of the city, this station serves as the center of the double Blue Loop known as the Resso Loop. 

### King Station
**Loop Service: Yellow**

Located inside one of the mountains within the [[Nevalene Mountains | Nevalene Mountain Range]], this station served as the starting point for the Orange Ways in the Federal District.

### Arisurn Station
**Loop Service: Yellow**

Located at the south of the Upper Loop, this station is the primary point of entry to the Divine Loop.

### Ronto Station
**Loop Service: Green | Yellow**

Located on the border of  the Holy District and Western Hills District, this station connects the Upper and West Hills Loops.

### Woul Station
**Loop Service: Red | Yellow**

Located just souht of the intersection between the Holy, Federal, and Tonamoye Districts, this Tonamoye station connects the Upper and Central Loops.

# Description
**AASIMAR**, or **demigods** are direct descendants of the Gods. They possess the power of their parent, but also take on their own power. 

Aasimar are considered [[Lesser Deities]] and are Gods in their own way. They age at a slower rate and are all but immortal to the consequences of time. However, unlike [[Greater Deities]], they can be killed by mortal means.
